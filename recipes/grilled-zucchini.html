Grilled Zucchini

Credit: Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

Description

Grilled zucchini makes the perfect summer side dish. This recipe is easy to prep with sliced zucchini and simple pantry ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchinis, quartered lengthwise
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder cheese
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat an outdoor grill for medium-low heat and lightly oil the grate.
  3. Brush zucchini with olive oil. Sprinkle Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and salt over zucchini.
  4. Cook on the preheated grill until charred and beginning to turn golden brown, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Brush balsamic vinegar over zucchini and continue cooking 1 minute more.
  5. Serve immediately.
